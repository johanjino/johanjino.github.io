The project

For Imperial's second-year engineering design project, we built an autonomous rover that balances on two wheels and drives itself through a maze marked out with white LED strips.

How it works

  • The rover keeps itself upright on two wheels while it explores.
  • As it drives, it records the maze and plots it in a web app.
  • Once the maze is mapped, the app finds the shortest path through it.
